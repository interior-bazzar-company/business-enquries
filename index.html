Common questions

The short honest answers.

Is this really free? What's the catch?

Free for you, yes. Interior bazzar is paid by the businesses on our platform for the service of receiving matched enquiries — you never pay us anything, and the business quotes you directly for your project.

Who will contact me, and how?

One verified interior business matched to your city and project type, usually by phone call or WhatsApp. Our team may also call once to confirm your requirement before matching.

Will my number be sold to many contractors?

No. Your enquiry goes to Interior bazzar and the matched business. We don't broadcast enquiries to lists of contractors — that's the exact problem this platform was built to avoid.

What if I'm only exploring and not ready to start?

That's fine — choose "Just exploring" in the form. We pass your stage along so the conversation matches where you actually are.

I run an interior business and need a designer — is this for me?

Yes. Choose "Designer for my business" in the form and tell us what you need — full-time, project-based or drawing support — and we'll connect you with a suitable professional from our network.

I'm an interior business — how do I receive enquiries like these?

Interior bazzar registers verified interior businesses and routes matched client enquiries to them. Write to us at support@interiorbazzar.com and our team will walk you through registration.
